Congratulations on the release! I found enough ROM-level information to give us both the immediate route to Charlock and a reliable blueprint for decoding the dungeon.

## Rainbow Bridge to Charlock

The Rainbow Drop is used at world coordinate `(65,49)`. The bridge appears immediately west at `(64,49)`, while Charlock’s entrance is `(48,48)`.

I decoded this shortest route from the original world-map data:

```text
West 4
South 1
West 3
South 8
West 2
South 6
West 5
North 2
West 1
North 1
West 1
North 10
East 2
North 2
West 3
North 1
```

That is 52 steps and 15 direction changes, ending at `(48,48)`. It includes 15 unavoidable swamp squares. Erdrick’s Armor makes the player immune to swamp and barrier damage and restores one HP per step. [[World-map ROM data](https://github.com/nmikstas/dragon-warrior-disassembly/blob/master/source_files/Bank00.asm)](https://github.com/nmikstas/dragon-warrior-disassembly/blob/master/source_files/Bank00.asm), [[Erdrick’s Armor details](https://guides.gamercorner.net/dw/arms/erdricks-armor)](https://guides.gamercorner.net/dw/arms/erdricks-armor)

This is a static route inferred from the original ROM map, so we should still validate it through the mod’s live movement executor.

## Castle entrance and secret passage

Charlock’s ground floor is a deliberate trap:

* Entrance: `(10,19)`
* Throne: `(10,3)` — searching gives the wind-behind-the-throne clue
* Real hidden stair: `(10,1)` — SEARCH here reveals it
* Western decoy stair: `(4,14)`
* Eastern decoy stair: `(15,14)`
* Locked decoy doors: `(4,8)` and `(15,8)`

The visible stairs lead into disconnected B1 branches. The actual dungeon route begins behind the throne. The throne area is approached through the western side of the castle. [[Charlock 1F guide](https://guides.gamercorner.net/dw/locations/charlock-castle-1f)](https://guides.gamercorner.net/dw/locations/charlock-castle-1f), [[ROM map-entry and search logic](https://github.com/nmikstas/dragon-warrior-disassembly/blob/master/source_files/Bank03.asm)](https://github.com/nmikstas/dragon-warrior-disassembly/blob/master/source_files/Bank03.asm)

For accessibility, I would treat `(10,1)` as a special searchable landmark rather than merely a staircase object: before SEARCH it is ordinary floor; afterward it becomes a usable stair.

## The B4 split and looping trap

B4 is the crucial routing decision:

* Arrive at B4 `(0,9)`.
* The western/northwestern stair at `(2,2)` is the correct Dragonlord route.
* The stair at `(7,7)` begins the optional route back upward toward Erdrick’s Sword.
* The northeastern stair at `(8,1)` begins the false inner route.

The false route continues through B5 `(5,5)` into B6 `(0,0)`. On that isolated B6 section, the stair at `(9,0)` sends the player straight back to `(0,0)` on the same floor, creating the famous infinite loop. The player must backtrack upward to escape.

Erdrick’s Sword is in a chest at B2 `(5,5)`, reached through the separate upward branch rather than the main descent. [[Erdrick’s Sword walkthrough](https://guides.gamercorner.net/dw/walkthrough/erdricks-sword)](https://guides.gamercorner.net/dw/walkthrough/erdricks-sword), [[B4 guide](https://guides.gamercorner.net/dw/location-floors/charlock-castle-b4)](https://guides.gamercorner.net/dw/location-floors/charlock-castle-b4)

## Final lair

The final map is fully lit even though B1–B6 are dark.

* Entry stair: `(10,29)`
* Locked treasure-room door: `(17,12)`
* Six treasure chests:

  * `(11,11)`
  * `(11,12)`
  * `(12,12)`
  * `(11,13)`
  * `(12,13)`
  * `(13,13)`
* Contents: two Herbs, `500–755` gold, one Magic Key, Wings, and a Cursed Belt
* The treasure room is optional; opening it consumes one key but supplies a replacement.
* Dragonlord is reached by following the outer path west, north, east, then south across the broken ground.
* Accepting his offer to “join” ends the run and requires a reset; refusing starts the two consecutive boss forms. [[Final-lair guide](https://guides.gamercorner.net/dw/location-floors/charlock-castle-b7)](https://guides.gamercorner.net/dw/location-floors/charlock-castle-b7)

Some map sites call this “Level 8” because they count the ground floor as Level 1. GamerCorner calls it B7. For the mod, I recommend `Ground Floor`, `B1`–`B6`, and `Dragonlord’s Lair/B7`.

## Important repository finding

The current [[objects.lua](https://github.com/techdog420/DragonWarriorBlindAccess-Public/blob/eaac61ff9f564b3117680ef5b1523002ad5f7d24/objects.lua)](https://github.com/techdog420/DragonWarriorBlindAccess-Public/blob/eaac61ff9f564b3117680ef5b1523002ad5f7d24/objects.lua) correctly warns that the existing deeper Charlock entries are provisional. Coordinates such as the current `(7,7)` and `(3,3)` descent chain should not be treated as canonical; the ROM stair table above gives the actual graph.

There also appear to be two map-numbering namespaces in the project:

* ROM/map-data IDs: `0x02`, `0x0F`–`0x14`, then `0x06`
* Older `$0045` runtime notes: `0x01`–`0x07` and `0x16`

We should log live `$0045`, X, and Y after every staircase before binding floor names. The namespaces may both be legitimate at different layers, but should not be merged by assumption.
