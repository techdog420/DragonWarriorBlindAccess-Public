This directory contains historical investigation handoffs, plus the detailed
roadmap and handoff retired after the 2026-09-24 map-cleanup pass.

These files are preserved as evidence, not active context.
In particular, `TASKS_2026-09-24_pre_cleanup.md` and
`session_handoff_2026-09-24_pre_cleanup.md` are verbatim snapshots. Their
branch, publication, test-count, and "next work" statements are dated claims,
not current instructions.

Use them when:

- Reconstructing an old survey or blocker-discovery sequence
- Auditing a historical route, seam, trap cell, or command-timing issue
