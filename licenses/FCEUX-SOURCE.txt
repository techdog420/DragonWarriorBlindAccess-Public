Corresponding source for bundled copyleft components
====================================================

The player package includes a modified FCEUX 2.6.6 executable, Qt 5.15.2
libraries, and the NVDA Controller Client 2026.1.1 library.

Complete corresponding source is distributed at no charge as the sibling release asset:

Dragon-Warrior-Accessibility-{{RELEASE_VERSION}}-Corresponding-Source.zip

Obtain that asset from the same download page as the player package. It
contains the patched FCEUX source and build instructions, the exact Qt source
archives used by the bundled Qt libraries, and the exact NVDA source snapshot
for the controller client. No upstream account or payment is required.
